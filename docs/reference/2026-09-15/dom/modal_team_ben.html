Bio

Ben Herman is the Founder of Aspen Search, the premier executive search firm for quantitative trading and technology talent. With over two decades of experience in recruiting, Ben has built a reputation as one of the most knowledgeable and results-driven talent partners in the industry.
Ben started his first executive search firm at 21 and never looked back. Over his career, he has personally billed $4.6M in a single year, generated over $150M in revenue from his first business, raised $96M from top-tier investors including Sequoia Capital, and angel invested in four unicorn companies. He has placed over 200 engineers and researchers at some of the most prestigious firms in quantitative finance, including Jane Street, Hudson River Trading, Two Sigma, PDT Partners, Citadel, and Headlands Technologies.
Today, Ben leads Aspen Search — formerly Herman Search — where he and his team focus on sourcing elite engineering, research, and AI talent for the world's top quantitative trading firms and VC-backed startups. His approach is built on deep relationships, radical honesty, and a genuine passion for connecting exceptional people with the right opportunities.
Outside of work, Ben is a proud dad of four, a devoted husband, and an avid collector of trading cards and sports memorabilia.
